DEEP BRAIN STIMULATION

Oct 2026 – present
Human microelectrode recordings | Milosevic et al. 2021 dataset

The same electrical pulse quiets some brain regions and excites others. I rebuilt the spike-detection pipeline to measure how each region responds as stimulation speeds up.

A Python pipeline that cleans stimulation artifacts, fixes mislabelled files and turns 749 raw recordings into per-neuron firing rates.

115 NEURONS · 749 RECORDINGS · 1–100 Hz

The neurons flash at rates scaled from my results: median firing rate as a share of baseline, for the 61 neurons that have a baseline recording.
